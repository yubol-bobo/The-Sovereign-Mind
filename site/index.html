TheSovereignMind

AI can search, summarize and analyze for me. But my attention, my judgment, my taste and my answers about life must be my own. The method is reading: borrowing decades of another person’s experience, pain and wisdom to see myself and the world anew.

05Add a star

Recommend a good book

This library welcomes books from anyone. A book that meets at least two of the criteria below belongs here.

Density of thoughtAfter reading it, you see the world through a lens you can never take off.
Density of beautyThe sentences themselves are worth reading slowly, rereading and copying out.
Density of lifeA real or fictional person lays a whole life open before you.

Not accepted: bestsellers with one idea stretched over three hundred pages, chicken soup for the soul, “Understand Y in X minutes”.
